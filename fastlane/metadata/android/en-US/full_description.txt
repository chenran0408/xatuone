XATU One is an Android campus app for students at Xi’an Technological University. Requires Android 12 or later.

Features:
• Daily and weekly schedules, course import and class reminders.
• Grades, credits, exams and academic progress.
• Academic calendars, electricity queries and campus web services.
• Home screen widgets, timetable customization, JSON import/export and ICS export, and WebDAV backups.
• Material 3, MIUIX and COUI appearance options with light, dark and system theme modes.

Academic and campus services require a valid account. Some services require the campus network. Available information depends on the university systems.
